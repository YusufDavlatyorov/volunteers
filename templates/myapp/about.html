{# ===== Location privacy (feature 7) — kept from the old page: a retention promise; plain-text data-i18n items so the RU pre-render covers them. ===== #}
{% include "myapp/about/_icon.html" with name="shield-check" %}
Privacy

Location privacy

  • A volunteer's location is shared only during an active request, and only after the volunteer agrees.
  • Only the client of that request and the curators can see it.
  • Sharing stops automatically when the request is completed or cancelled, and the volunteer can stop it at any time.
  • The route trail is deleted when the request ends and is never kept longer than 24 hours.
  • Chat photos are deleted 90 days and chat messages 180 days after the request ends.
